The Emberrun

The The Emberrun series by Raven DeNoir: 2 books in reading order. Start with Marked by Flames 2nd Edition.

The Emberrun is a dark, shifter-paranormal BWWM romance set in a cursed Vermont mountain town where the forest watches and the wolves keep score. Three Hollowborn alphas bound by fire, one Solenya who was never meant to survive, fated mates, primal rituals, found family, and a heroine who refuses to bow, even to destiny.
