The Don: King Takes Queen

by Raven DeNoir · So, About Last Night, Book 1

He left to protect her.

She spent seven years deciding what to do about that.

When Reyna Dorne finds a key charm in her mailbox on her daughter's seventh birthday, one word engraved on the silver, she understands exactly what Vadim Dragunov has been trying to say. ВСЕГДА. Always.

She is done waiting for always.

Armed with seven years of evidence, a forensic accountant, and the legal precision that has defined her entire career, Reyna goes looking for the door the key opens. What she finds is a man who never stopped watching. A threat he has not yet finished dismantling. And a collision that has been building since the night she walked out of his hotel room and rebuilt her life from scratch.

But Vadim is not the only one who has been paying attention.
