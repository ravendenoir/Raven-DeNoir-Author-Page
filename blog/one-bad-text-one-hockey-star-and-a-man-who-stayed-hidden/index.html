One Bad Text, One Hockey Star, and a Man Who Stayed Hidden

A woman texts the wrong man. Two strangers meet on a flight to Iceland. A professional hockey player meets a veterinarian who isn't impressed by his smile. And before the month is over, a man who's spent years staying invisible is going to have to step into the light.

You know how I write these men. They can be powerful, rich, dangerous, famous, or every damn one of those things. The woman still gets to decide whether he's worth her time.

Let's talk books.

Two new nights to remember

On November 6, two more stories joined my So, About Last Night series.

The Wrong Number | Book Eleven

Leslie O'Neill sends a message meant for someone else. Christopher recognizes her immediately, but she has no idea who's really answering.

He's forty-seven, successful, accustomed to having control, and about to make a spectacular mess of his personal life over one text message.

She's got her own business, her own opinions, and no reason to make things easy for him when the truth comes out.

One wrong number. A very complicated right person.

**Read The Wrong Number**

The Destination | Book Twelve

Sadia boards a flight to Iceland carrying grief she hasn't had time to deal with. Kelvar, the Icelandic man seated beside her, recognizes something in the way she's holding herself together.

What starts as a conversation between strangers becomes a connection neither planned for.

This one takes us from a plane leaving New York into the cold, extraordinary landscape of Iceland, with two adults who have more to lose than they're ready to admit.

**Read The Destination**

A hockey player meets his match

Fair Play, Power Play, Breakaway released November 20, and Cole Ransom has a problem.

Her name is Dr. Bree Calloway.

Cole is a six-foot-seven professional hockey forward, and Bree is a veterinarian who has considerably more important things to do than entertain a handsome athlete.

Their first encounter involves a dog named Björn, a bee sting, and a backyard cookout. Cole is interested almost immediately.

Bree is considerably harder to impress.

This story brings sports romance, sharp chemistry, an independent Black heroine, and a man who has to learn that wanting a woman and earning her trust are two different things.

**Read Fair Play, Power Play, Breakaway**

Coming November 27: Out of the Smoke

Now we're going somewhere darker.

Ori Daniels has spent her adult life becoming someone nobody can find. She's meticulous, independent, and very good at moving through dangerous places without leaving a trace.

Mateo Maros has spent years watching over her from a distance. He's carrying scars, secrets, and the kind of devotion that comes with a cost.

But when Ori's enemies begin closing in from several directions, his old methods stop being enough. She needs answers, and Mateo is going to have to give her more than protection.

Out of the Smoke, Book One in Kept, is dark romantic suspense about a dangerous past, hard choices, and two people learning what trust actually requires.

Releases November 27, 2026.
